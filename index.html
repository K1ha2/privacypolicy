Privacy Policy

Protecting your data with care and transparency

🇬🇧 English

We take your privacy seriously. If you use our app to make a payment and enter your banking or payment details, this data is used solely to process and secure the transaction. This includes actions such as account verification or integration with trusted payment processors like Stripe Financial Connections.

We do not store complete banking information on our servers. All processing is performed through certified third-party providers, in compliance with GDPR regulations. Your data is never shared unless strictly necessary to fulfill the transaction.
